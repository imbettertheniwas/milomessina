04 — the rules

What counts,
and what does not.

One referrer per person

The first link somebody opens is the one that holds. If two people send the same friend a link, the earlier one is credited — no splitting, no arguing about it afterwards.

You cannot refer yourself

A code used on its own owner's application is dropped. So are duplicate accounts for the same person, which is the other half of the same rule.

The rungs stack

One person can pay you more than once. Somebody who joins their clan, then gets approved as a creator, then gets hired pays out at each rung as they reach it.

Completion is checked by a person

Every payout is reviewed before it is released. It is a quick look, not an ordeal — but it means nothing pays out on a form somebody filled in twice.

Money goes to your fomo account

Not a bank transfer, not a cheque. It lands in the fomo account behind the username you claimed with, which is why that username has to be one that actually exists.

We can close it

Bought traffic, fake signups, or people who did not agree to be signed up ends the code and anything unpaid on it. It has to be said, and it is the only thing here that ever gets anyone removed.

Referral payouts are separate from what the person you referred earns. Their $500 chapter payout, their $25 creator bonus and their pay per view are theirs in full — nothing is taken out of it to pay you.
