What happens next.

Four steps, and you hear from us at every one of them. Nothing here depends on you chasing us.

  1. 01

    You apply

    Link your TikTok if you’ve made UGC ads before. Otherwise, send a concept video explaining your ideas and what you can create on campus. Make sure we can open your link.

    same minute
  2. 02

    We review it by hand

    We review your previous UGC ads or your concept and email you a decision within five business days. If approved, you receive $25 at approval and can start submitting original TikTok videos for pay per view. The approval bonus does not wait for the 30-day video measurement period.

    within 5 business days
  3. 03

    You post, we count

    After creator approval, post videos that meet the requirements above and send us each link. For each approved post, we read its total qualifying view count 30 days after submission, including eligible organic views it already had. Paid promotion and purchased views do not count. Cross-posted videos, reposts, and repeat submissions are ineligible.

    30 days after each post submission
  4. 04

    You get paid

    The video lands in the next payout after day 30, on the 1st or the 15th, sent to the payment account you gave us and itemised so you can see what this video earned.

    1st or 15th
