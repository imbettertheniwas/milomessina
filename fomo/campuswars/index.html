What makes my house grow?+

Your chapter builds its house at 15 members. Reach 80% of your active roster to unlock a private backyard with a pool, stone terrace, loungers and landscaping. Every chapter can earn it. Your house keeps its chapter style and grows with its rank by members onboarded; the backyard appears automatically as live registrations reach the goal.

How does the village connect to Greek Wars?+

Greek Wars is a semester-long trading competition on fomo. Your Greek chapter is your team, and its house in the village shows its onboarding progress. Chapters compete at their school and nationally, ranked on raw PnL—profit and loss in dollars, not trading activity. The village’s leaderboard measures member signups, not trading performance. The national trading prize amount will be announced later.

How do I join a chapter that’s already here?+

Ask your chapter lead for your chapter’s invite link. Joining through that link counts toward the roster’s 80% target. Your house and the row’s standings update automatically as registrations arrive.

Who gets the $500—and the extra $250?+

Every chapter that onboards at least 80% of its active members qualifies for $500, paid to the chapter. The first chapter at each school to reach 80% earns an extra $250. Fraternities and sororities compete for the same first-chapter bonus. Registering alone does not qualify your chapter.

Can sororities compete too?+

Yes. Recognized Greek chapters can enter, including fraternities and sororities. Members competing must be 18 or older.

When does it end?+

The listed Fall 2026 season runs August 24–December 11. Trading involves risk, including loss of principal. Competition prizes are not guaranteed trading returns.
