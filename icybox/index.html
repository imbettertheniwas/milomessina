A few things to know.

Does everyone get a box?+

Each milestone reward is for the sorority chapter, rather than one box per member.

What counts as onboarding?+

The program team confirms qualifying activity through your IcyBox referral code. This page prepares your invite; it doesn’t track members or award boxes.

Who can join?+

Members must be 18+ and meet IcyBox’s eligibility requirements. Check the app’s current terms for purchase, shipping and redemption conditions.
