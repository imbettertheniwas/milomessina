What is IcyBox?+

IcyBox is an app for digital mystery boxes containing physical luxury items, including watches and bags. You can explore the available boxes and item details in the app.

Do I need a student email?+

No student email is needed to explore this page or create a campus card. Account eligibility and purchase requirements are set by IcyBox in the app.

Are the items physical products?+

Yes. The digital reveal represents a physical item. Delivery options, timing and any available sell-back offers are explained in the app.

Is this a student discount program?+

This page is a campus-inspired introduction to IcyBox. It does not offer a student discount or promise campus-specific rewards.

What should I know before buying?+

You must be at least 18 and meet IcyBox's eligibility requirements. Boxes are paid purchases with varying outcomes. Read the current item odds, fees, sale terms and applicable restrictions before making a purchase.
