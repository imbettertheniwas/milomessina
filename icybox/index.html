04 / BEFORE THE GROUP CHAT ASKS

Good questions.
Clear answers.

What does our sorority get?+

Your chapter’s reward milestones are a Wonder Box at 10 onboarded members, an Everyday Box at 20, a Runway Box at 50, and a Dream Box at 150.

Does every member get a box?+

The milestone reward is for your sorority chapter, rather than a separate box for every member.

How do we invite our members?+

The chapter portal guides you to your IcyBox referral code, then creates a message you can copy straight into your sorority’s group chat.

Which bags can be inside?+

Choose a box in the “What’s inside” section. Only a verified item list will be shown for that box. Until its contents are confirmed, use the in-app list. The separate catalog carousel is inspiration, not a box-specific prize pool.

How are member counts confirmed?+

Use your IcyBox referral code for invites. Opening a link or moving the preview slider does not count as onboarding. Confirm qualifying referral activity and reward eligibility with the program team.

Who can participate?+

IcyBox is for adults 18 and older. Members must meet the app’s eligibility requirements. Review the current terms and any purchase, shipping or redemption conditions in the app.
