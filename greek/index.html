the quick answers

no guesswork.

Why be the first chapter at my school?+

Onboarding is just the first step. We’re planning to spend a lot more on your campus throughout the year. The first chapter to onboard gets to direct where that campus capital goes.

Who gets the $6.25?+

The first person who sets up and onboards their chapter gets $6.25 per onboarded member in their own fomo account.

Do I need the whole chapter before I get paid?+

No. Get a batch of brothers fully onboarded, get paid, and keep going. For example, 30 brothers earns you $187.50. Another 100 earns you another $625. There’s no 80% threshold.

What does every brother need to do?+

Every brother must individually fill out the chapter tracker and join fomo through the Rewards link of the brother who registered the chapter. Both are required. We match his tracker entry to his fomo signup, add him to the chapter’s clan, and pay the organizer $6.25.

Who creates our clan?+

We do. Our team creates your chapter’s clan and adds your members. Just share the tracker and your fomo Rewards link. You don’t need to create a clan or invite members yourself.

Which link goes in the group chat?+

Send both your chapter tracker link and your personal fomo Rewards link. Find the second link under Profile → Rewards and tap the share icon. Every brother fills out the tracker and joins fomo through the Rewards link. We match both before paying you.

Why does the Rewards screen say 25%?+

The app’s Rewards screen shows a separate reward based on friends’ trading fees. This campus offer pays you $6.25 per onboarded member. Referring a new chapter organizer pays $50 for a chapter under 60 members, $75 for 60–110, or $100 for over 110. That reward is extra, paid by fomo.

Do I have to deposit anything?+

No. You don’t need to make a deposit to receive your onboarding payout. Get brothers fully onboarded, then get paid straight to your fomo account. You can do it in batches, with no extra waiting period.

Does a referral reduce my payout?+

No. You keep the full $6.25 per onboarded member. If someone referred you, fomo pays them separately after your chapter onboards: $50 for a chapter under 60 members, $75 for 60–110, or $100 for over 110.
