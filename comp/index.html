03 / THE DETAILS

The rules. Plain and simple.

Everything your chapter needs to know.

When does it run?

The competition starts October 31, 2026 at 12:00 AM New York time and ends December 25, 2026 at 12:00 AM New York time—the start of Christmas Day. Entry closes at that same Christmas cutoff.

You can register and fund your accounts before Halloween. Members can also join during the competition, before entry closes.

Who qualifies, and who counts?

Your chapter needs at least 25 registered members, each with at least $5 funded in their own fomo account. You don’t need to onboard your entire chapter. Existing users qualify if they already have at least $5 in their account.

Once your chapter qualifies, all funded members count automatically, including members beyond the first 25. Each funded member’s percentage return is weighted equally. Unfunded accounts don’t count.

Each person can compete through only one account and for only one chapter.

What if I don’t trade—or I lose money?

If you deposit but never trade, your account still contributes to your chapter’s average return. Any changes in the value of existing holdings count too.

A member who loses money cannot leave the chapter’s score. Their performance stays included.

Can I join late, add money, or withdraw?

Yes. Members can join, deposit more, or withdraw after Halloween. Entry closes at the Christmas cutoff.

Deposits and withdrawals don’t change performance. Adding money isn’t a gain, and taking money out isn’t a loss.

If you join after Halloween, your return begins from your first deposit.

Which trades, holdings, and fees count?

Members can trade any assets available on fomo. Results combine the performance of members’ individual fomo accounts.

Existing holdings count, but only profit or loss (PnL) generated during the Halloween-to-Christmas competition window counts. Gains and losses from before Halloween are excluded.

Unsold gains and losses count toward the final score. Returns are measured after fees only if the asset is sold to cash.

What if everyone loses? What if there’s a tie?

The chapter with the highest average percentage return wins. If every chapter loses money, the chapter with the smallest percentage loss wins.

If there is a tie for first, the $100,000 prize is split between the tied chapters.

Is the prize guaranteed? Who gets paid?

The $100,000 prize is guaranteed regardless of participation.

The prize is paid to the designated chapter point of contact (POC)—the person who onboarded the fraternity. That person receives the prize, controls its use, and chooses what to do with it. In a tie, each winning chapter’s share is paid to its designated POC.
