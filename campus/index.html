READING THE MODEL

A score you can explain.

The Campus Opportunity Score is a weighted average of observed, normalized metrics. Missing metrics are omitted from that average and reduce completeness. It is not a prediction of campaign performance.

Observed score = Σ(observed weight × metric score) / Σ(observed weight)

The evidence floor keeps the full weight denominator. Its companion upper bound allows every missing normalized metric to be 100. The range shows unresolved score potential; it is not a confidence interval.

How the starting universe was selected

Union of the 2027-edition Niche top 50 social/party schools and The Princeton Review’s 25 “Lots of Greek Life” schools, as accessed September 29, 2026. Resolve to NCES UNITIDs; retain institutions with at least 5,000 undergraduates; select the largest 50. University FSL offices provide independent primary-source evidence. List positions do not contribute to opportunity scores.

What the data can support

Comparable federal enrollment, demographics and degree-award measures; university-published Greek-life facts; airport distance and GSA lodging proxies. Documented content programs and university-advertised content opportunities provide a limited recruitment proxy. There is no audited creator census, client customer footprint, or historical campaign dataset.

Dates, scope and confidence

Enrollment and demographics use Fall 2023 IPEDS extracts; bachelor awards cover 2023–24. Greek observations retain their own periods and source qualifiers. Confidence is a transparent source-quality, freshness and proxy-specificity rubric—not a statistical probability. WSU’s systemwide student measures are excluded from Pullman scoring.

Repeatable by design

Fixed anchors prevent a new school from changing every existing score. The scoring engine is shared by this dashboard, the export pipeline and the tests. Add a UNITID, refresh public extracts, append reviewed evidence, and rebuild.
