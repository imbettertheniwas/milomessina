## Remaining gaps

Client customer and campaign measurements require attributable client exports. Creator density requires a comparable student-creator census. Event costs and lead times require a defined activation brief, travel origin/dates and vendor or campus quotes. Most public undergraduate-resident counts were not verified; bed capacity is not substituted. Some Greek participation denominators remain unresolved, including Arizona’s 51,942-student denominator. WSU system enrollment remains excluded from Pullman scoring.

This is a documented recovery pass, not a claim that every public source has been exhausted. No missing value was fabricated or replaced with zero.
