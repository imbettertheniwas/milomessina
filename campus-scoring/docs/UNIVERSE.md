# Initial universe audit

Accessed 2026-09-29. Discovery is the union of two independent student-review publishers, followed by an official NCES enrollment filter. Discovery rank is never a score input.

- [Niche 2027 party/social top 50, page 1](https://www.niche.com/colleges/search/top-party-schools/) and [page 2](https://www.niche.com/colleges/search/top-party-schools/?page=2).
- [The Princeton Review 2027 Lots of Greek Life, 25 schools](https://www.princetonreview.com/college-rankings?rankings=lots-greek-life).
- [NCES EF2023A enrollment](https://nces.ed.gov/ipeds/datacenter/data/ef2023a.zip), matched by UNITID.

Initial union: 68 institutions; 53 had at least 5,000 undergraduates; the largest 50 became the release universe. Fifteen were below threshold; three eligible institutions fell below the size cutoff. The table preserves all 68 candidates. Later client-requested additions need not appear on either discovery list and are retained with their reason.

WSU selection used its published institutional headcount, but this is a multi-campus scope. It is retained as a flagged discovery candidate and excluded from Pullman population/program scoring; the model does not pass that system count off as Pullman enrollment.
